About this map

Method

  • Audio is the first 1.5 h after sunset at one recorder per A2O site, sampled one night a week (every night at Fowlers Gap and, more densely, at six south-eastern sites), all years pooled.
  • Most A2O audio is 22.05 kHz, so only the bottom of this bat's ~11–15 kHz call, a ~10 kHz tail, is recorded. A signal-processing pulse detector finds it; an A2O-trained Perch classifier confirms the detector's candidate windows.
  • A fortnight with no audio is shown as no data, never as absence. Shares resting on few dusks are noisy; the number sampled is in every tooltip.
  • Other free-tailed bats in northern Australia call in the same band. Northern detections are provisional until listened to.
